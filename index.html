🌍 World Live TV, Sports, Cartoons & Power Rangers for Stremio

Self-hosted, static live TV, sports, telenovelas, and on-demand catalog add-ons for Stremio. Free-to-Air, major sports (F1, AFL, UFC, Boxing, NFL, NBA, MLB, Soccer), 24/7 Toonertia & Spelunkr TV retro cartoon/anime channels (spelunkr.tv/toonertia), 24/7 Telenovelas, a massive 661-title Cartoon & Animation Vault catalog, and the complete Power Rangers Universe (all 30 seasons across every era & movies).

VPN & Stream Playback

Power Rangers Universe and Cartoon & Animation Vault are static on-demand catalogs referencing canonical IMDb metadata via Cinemeta. They work worldwide without a VPN; clicking any series or movie loads all seasons/episodes and streams seamlessly via your installed stream addons (Torrentio, RealDebrid, etc.).

Free-to-Air channels (like BBC, ITV in the UK; ABC, Seven, Nine, 10 in Australia; TVNZ in New Zealand; RTÉ in Ireland) require you to be physically located in that country or have a VPN connected to that country.

FAST & News channels (Pluto TV, Samsung TV Plus, CBS News, Sky News, Al Jazeera, DW, Red Bull TV) generally work openly worldwide.

Stremio Web (Brave / Browser) vs Native Apps

Each channel provides two streams:
• Live (browser): Use this in Stremio Web (e.g. web.stremio.com in Brave) when the Stremio Desktop app or Stremio Service is not running on your computer. (If Stremio Desktop is running in the background, Stremio Web hands the stream to the local desktop server where live HLS redirects fail—so either quit Stremio Desktop when using the browser, or watch inside the Stremio Desktop app directly.)
• Live: Use this in the Stremio Desktop, Android TV, and mobile apps.
• Updating an existing addon: Stremio caches the manifest it saw at install time. If you installed an older version previously, Uninstall it in Stremio first, then click Install again.

Using with AIOStreams

To bundle any country into your AIOStreams setup: In AIOStreams go to Addons → Marketplace → Custom, paste the country's manifest link, click Install, make sure the catalog is active, and click Save Configuration.
